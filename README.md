At some point I decided TV wasn't it any longer. I decided I needed to live and breathe YouTube. So I turned myself into a YouTube addict.

Now that I watch a lot more YouTube/Netflix/Amazon Prime, and I realized I can use AI to compile a basic list for me
I decided to give it a try.

Though I use AI tools to generate the list, this is a semi hand-curated list of things I've been watching. 
I sometimes let my YouTube player run wild while I sleep, and my watch history gets a bit overrun with videos I have no clue about. I removed those.
Also,  sometimes I inadvertently click on random videos. I removed those.
And finally, sometimes YouTube just likes to sneak in videos that I _KNOW_ I didn't watch into my history. I removed those too.

This is nothing more than a dump of the videos I clicked, and hopefully it doesn't make you judget who I am as a person :)
